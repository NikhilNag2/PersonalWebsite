Why Claude Can't Catch Its Own Mistakes (And the Fix)

🐦 Fresh Eyes: Never Let Claude Grade Its Own Homework

💡 The one rule that fixes 90% of AI mistakes: stop asking the same chat to check its own work. It has the exact same blind spots that caused the error in the first place.

🎯 The Problem in One Sentence

When you tell a chat "go back and double-check that," it re-reads its own output through the same flawed lens. It's proofreading your own email at 2am — you'll skim right past the typo every single time.

The fix isn't a smarter prompt. It's a new set of eyes.

🧠 What's a Sub-Agent?

A sub-agent is a brand new chat with a totally blank memory, spawned to review your work.

Same Chat ReviewSub-Agent Review
🧠 MemoryRemembers its own answerZero context — clean slate
👀 BiasDefends its first takeNo ego, no attachment
🎯 Catches errorsRarelyConsistently
💪 CapabilityFullFull (it's just another agent)
💡 Don't let the name fool you — "sub" doesn't mean weaker. It's a full-power agent that simply hasn't seen the work yet. Fresh eyes, same brainpower.

⚡ The 10-Second Version

Just type this after Claude finishes something:

Spawn this to a sub-agent with no prior context and tell me exactly what's wrong with it. Be specific.

That's it. The fresh agent will flag things the first one swore weren't there.

🔥 The Real Hack: Spawn a Panel

You're not limited to one reviewer. Spin up three sub-agents with different personas, have each work blind, and collect three independent opinions.

Spawn 3 separate sub-agents to review the work above. 
Each must operate independently and CANNOT see the others' responses.

Agent 1 — Skeptical Customer: pick apart what you don't buy, 
what's confusing, and where you'd click away.

Agent 2 — Sharp Copywriter: flag weak lines, boring phrasing, 
and where the hook or flow drags.

Agent 3 — Sales Strategist: judge whether this actually 
drives action and where the persuasion breaks down.

Each agent returns their TOP 3 changes only. 
No overlap-checking, no consensus — I want raw, honest takes.

Now you've got three unfiltered perspectives that can't influence each other. That's how you kill your own echo chamber.

📚 Prompt Library

🔍 Single fresh-eyes review
Spawn a sub-agent with zero prior context. 
Hand it the work above and ask it to find the 3 biggest 
problems a first-time viewer would notice. 
Rank them by impact.
🎭 Three-persona panel (blind review)
Spawn 3 independent sub-agents. They cannot see each other's output.
- Skeptical Customer: where do you lose trust or interest?
- Copywriter: where does the writing fall flat?
- Sales Expert: where does this fail to convert?
Each returns their top 3 fixes. No collaboration.
✅ Fact-check specialist
Spawn a sub-agent to fact-check the claims above. 
For each claim: mark it Verified, Unclear, or Likely False, 
and explain why in one line. Assume nothing — verify everything.
⚔️ Devil's advocate
Spawn a sub-agent whose ONLY job is to argue against 
the work above. Find every weak assumption and logical gap. 
Don't be polite — I want the strongest counter-case possible.
🎯 Prioritized action list
Spawn a sub-agent to review the work and return a single 
prioritized list: what to fix first, second, third. 
One sentence per item. No fluff.

💡 Pro Tips

🔧 Make personas match your actual audience

Swap the generic personas for the real people who judge your work — your ideal buyer, your toughest client, your pickiest editor. The closer the persona to reality, the sharper the feedback.

🔧 Keep the agents blind

Always specify that sub-agents cannot see each other's answers. The whole value is independent opinions. The moment they can peek, they start agreeing — and you're back in the echo chamber.

🔧 Cap the feedback

Ask for "top 3 changes" instead of "everything wrong." You'll get the highest-impact fixes instead of a wall of nitpicks you'll never action.

🚫 The One Rule

💡 Never let AI be your echo chamber. A single chat agreeing with itself feels great and teaches you nothing. Force a fresh, blind perspective — that's where the real catches come from.
