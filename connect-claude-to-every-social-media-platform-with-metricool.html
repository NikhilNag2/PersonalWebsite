⚙️ Setup in 5 Minutes

Step 1 — Create Your Metricool Account

  1. Go to metricool.com and create an account
  2. Click Add Brand in your dashboard
  3. Connect each social account: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, X, etc.
  4. Connect your ad accounts if you run Meta, Google, or TikTok Ads
  5. Add competitors in the Competitors section
💡 Tip: Connect ALL your accounts, even ones you don't post on frequently. Metricool collects analytics in the background so the data is ready when you need it.

Step 2 — Connect the Metricool MCP to Claude

On Claude.ai:

  1. Go to Settings → Connectors
  2. Click + → Add Custom Connector
  3. Name it Metricool
  4. Paste the MCP URL below
  5. Click Save and authorize your Metricool account
https://ai.metricool.com/mcp

On Claude Desktop:

  1. Open Settings → Developer → Edit Config
  2. Add the Metricool MCP server to your config file
  3. Save and restart Claude Desktop
🔌 Also works with: Cursor, ChatGPT (Developer Mode), Mistral Le Chat, Make, n8n, Smithery, and Gemini CLI — same URL, same setup.

Step 3 — Test the Connection

Show me all the brands connected to my Metricool account.

If Claude responds with your account data, you're all set.

🔒 Is Your Data Safe?

Yes. The MCP uses the same permissions and authentication as your Metricool account. Your data is fetched in real time, used to generate a response, and not retained by Claude. You can revoke access at any time from your Metricool settings.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Be specific with date ranges — say "the last 14 days" instead of "recently"
  • Always add "at my best posting time" when scheduling — it uses your real engagement data
  • Add 3–5 competitors immediately so Metricool starts collecting their data right away
  • Connect your ad accounts too — most people forget this and miss the full picture
  • Ask for explanations, not just data — "show me my analytics and explain what's working and what's not"
  • Check the content calendar after scheduling — Metricool's visual calendar is your confirmation layer
