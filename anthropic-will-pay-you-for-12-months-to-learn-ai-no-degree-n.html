✅ Eligibility Requirements

The barrier to entry is shockingly low. Here's all you need:

RequirementDetail

1️⃣ Check Your Eligibility

Confirm you're over 18 and have under two years of professional experience. That's the whole checklist.

💎 Pro Tips

🌟 Lead with potential, not pedigree

This program explicitly has no educational requirements. Don't undersell yourself because you lack a degree or experience — that's literally the point. Frame your application around curiosity, drive, and willingness to learn.

⚡ Move fast on opportunities like this

Programs from top AI companies fill up quickly. Bookmark the link, set a reminder for the deadline, and get your application materials ready before you need them.

🧩 Show you care about impact

The nonprofit placement is core to the program. Demonstrate that you're genuinely motivated to contribute to society, not just collect a credential. That alignment matters.

📈 Think long-term

The real prize here isn't just the 12 months of pay — it's the career trajectory. The AI skills you gain are some of the most competitive ones you can have right now. Treat this as a launchpad.

💡 Bottom line: One of the top AI companies in the world wants to invest in you, train you in elite skills, place you somewhere meaningful, and pay you the whole time. This is genuinely an opportunity of a lifetime — and the requirements are minimal. Don't sit on it.

🍀 Good luck.
