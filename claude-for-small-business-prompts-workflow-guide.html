Claude for Small Business — Prompts & Workflow Guide

🏪 What This Is

Claude for Small Business connects Claude directly into the tools you already use — QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365. It runs inside Claude Cowork with 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows and 15 built-in skills.

This guide gives you copy-paste prompts for each major use case so you can get results immediately.

💬 Copy-Paste Prompts by Use Case

💰 Finance — Payroll Planning
I need help planning payroll for this period. Here’s our current QuickBooks cash position: [PASTE BALANCE]. We have the following upcoming settlements from PayPal: [PASTE AMOUNTS + DATES]. Please:
1. Tell me if we have enough cash to cover payroll on [DATE]
2. Flag any gaps or timing issues
3. Draft a 30-day cash forecast
4. List any overdue payments I should chase first
📊 Finance — Month-End Close
Help me close this month’s books. I’ll paste in our QuickBooks transactions and PayPal settlement report. Please:
1. Reconcile and flag anything that doesn’t match
2. Write a plain-English summary of our P&L
3. Identify the top 3 expense categories driving cost this month
4. Draft a close packet summary I can forward to my accountant

[PASTE TRANSACTIONS / REPORT]
📅 Finance — Business Pulse Dashboard
Give me a business health snapshot for this week. Pull from what I share below and summarize:
- Cash position (from QuickBooks)
- Sales trend vs. last month
- Pipeline movement (from HubSpot)
- This week’s key commitments
- Any open invoices more than 14 days overdue

[PASTE DATA OR CONNECT INTEGRATIONS]
📣 Marketing — Run a Campaign
I want to run a promotional campaign. Here’s context:
- Business type: [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]
- Current slow period: [MONTH/SEASON]
- Past campaign performance from HubSpot: [PASTE METRICS]
- Target audience: [DESCRIBE CUSTOMER]

Please:
1. Recommend a promo strategy for this slow period
2. Draft the campaign messaging (email subject, body, CTA)
3. Suggest 3 Canva asset formats I should create
4. Give me a send schedule
🤝 Sales — Lead Triage
Here are my current leads from HubSpot. Please score and triage them:
[PASTE LEAD LIST OR EXPORT]

For each lead, tell me:
1. How likely they are to convert based on their activity
2. What stage of the pipeline they should be in
3. Recommended next action (call, email, proposal, etc.)
4. Which 3 leads I should prioritize this week and why
⚖️ Operations — Invoice Chaser
Here are my outstanding invoices: [PASTE INVOICE LIST WITH AMOUNTS + DUE DATES]

Please:
1. Flag any overdue by more than 7 days
2. Draft a polite but firm follow-up email for each overdue invoice
3. Suggest which ones to escalate and how
4. Tell me the total cash I’m owed and the expected collection timeline
⚖️ Operations — Contract Review
Review this contract before I sign it. Focus on:
1. Any unusual or unfavorable terms
2. Automatic renewal clauses
3. Liability or indemnification language
4. Payment terms and late fee provisions
5. Exit or termination conditions

Flag anything I should negotiate or ask a lawyer about.

[PASTE CONTRACT TEXT]
📉 Finance — Margin Analyzer
Analyze my profit margins for this period. Here’s our revenue and cost data:
[PASTE DATA]

Please:
1. Calculate gross and net margin by product/service line
2. Identify which offerings are most and least profitable
3. Flag any costs that seem out of proportion
4. Recommend 2–3 specific actions to improve margins this quarter
🧹 Operations — Tax Season Prep
Help me prepare for tax season. I’ll share our QuickBooks data and expense records. Please:
1. Categorize and summarize all deductible expenses
2. Flag any transactions that need receipts or documentation
3. Identify any missed deduction categories I should explore with my accountant
4. Give me a checklist of what to gather before our accountant meeting

[PASTE DATA]
