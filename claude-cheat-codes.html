🧠 15 Claude Cheat Codes That Actually Work

💡 These are prompt prefixes — paste them at the start of any Claude prompt to instantly change how it thinks.

🔥 The 5 From the Video

#CodeWhat It DoesBest Used For
1Pre-mortem:Assumes failure 6 months from now, works backwardPlans, launches, decisions
2Assume I'm wrong:Finds every flaw in your own position before you defend itArguments, strategies, beliefs you hold
3Red team this:Attacks your idea from every critic anglePitches, strategies, proposals
4Give me the 10/10 version:Rewrites your work at world-class levelEmails, plans, pitches, any writing
5Break this down from first principles:Strips assumptions, rebuilds from ground truthAny problem needing original thinking

➕ 10 More Secret Codes

#CodeWhat It DoesBest Used For
6ELI5:Explains like you're 5Complex topics: tax law, contracts, technical docs
7Devil's advocate:Takes the opposing stance, even if wrongTesting your reasoning
8Second-order effects:Thinks 2–3 steps beyond the obvious outcomeStrategy, policy, business decisions
9Invert this:Flips the problem — asks what would make it failProblem-solving, goal-setting
10Rubber duck this:Walks through your logic out loud to find the flawDebugging, stuck thinking
11As a skeptic:Responds with healthy doubt and challenges every claimFact-checking, research
12Compress this:Cuts to the absolute minimum — no fluffSummaries, TL;DRs, pitches
13Steel thread:Finds the single most important insight in a pile of infoResearch, long docs, meeting notes
14Taboo words: [X, Y, Z] —Explains something without using those wordsForcing clarity, teaching simply
15What would [person] say?Channels a specific thinker's perspectiveStrategy, creativity, leadership

📋 Copy-Paste Prompt Templates

💀 Pre-Mortem – Find What Will Kill Your Plan 
Pre-mortem: It's 6 months from now and [your project/plan] has completely failed. What went wrong? Work backwards and give me the top reasons it didn't work.

[paste your plan here]
🪞 Assume I'm Wrong – Find Your Own Flaws 
Assume I'm wrong: Here's my position — [your argument or belief]. Find every flaw, gap, or weak assumption in my thinking. Be direct.
🎯 Red Team – Attack Your Own Idea 
Red team this: Here's my plan/pitch/decision. Attack it from every angle a critic, investor, or skeptic would. Don't hold back.

[paste your plan here]
⭐ Give Me the 10/10 Version – Rewrite at World-Class Level 
Give me the 10/10 version: Here's my [email/plan/pitch]. Rewrite it as if a world-class expert wrote it from scratch. Don't just make it better — make it the best possible version.

[paste your draft here]
⚙️ First Principles – Strip It Back to Truth 
Break this down from first principles: [your question or problem]. Don't give me recycled advice — start from what's actually true and build up from there.
🔄 Invert This – Flip the Problem 
Invert this: Instead of asking how to achieve [goal], ask what would guarantee I fail at it — then reverse those answers.

[paste your goal here]
🧵 Steel Thread – Find the One Key Insight 
Steel thread: Read this and tell me the single most important insight I should take away. Cut everything else.

[paste content here]
🚫 Taboo Words – Force Clarity 
Taboo words: [word1, word2, word3] — Explain [topic] without using those words.

Example:

Taboo words: blockchain, decentralized, crypto — Explain how NFTs work.

⚡ Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

🔵 UNDERSTAND IT
ELI5:                          → Simplify anything
Think out loud:                → Show me the reasoning
Compress this:                 → Give me the TL;DR

🔴 STRESS TEST IT
Pre-mortem:                    → Find what kills your plan
Red team this:                 → Attack from all angles
Assume I'm wrong:              → Find flaws in my own thinking
Devil's advocate:              → Push back on my thinking

🟡 REFRAME IT
Break this down from first principles:   → Original thinking only
Invert this:                             → What would guarantee failure?
Second-order effects:                    → What happens after what happens?

🟢 FIND THE SIGNAL
Steel thread:                  → One key insight from everything
Rubber duck this:              → Walk through my logic
As a skeptic:                  → Challenge every claim

🟣 UPGRADE IT
Give me the 10/10 version:     → World-class rewrite of anything
What would [person] say?       → See through a different mind
Taboo words: [X] —             → Explain without those words
🎯 Pro tip: Stack these codes. Try: "Pre-mortem + first principles: Here's my startup idea..." — you'll get a depth of analysis most people never see.
