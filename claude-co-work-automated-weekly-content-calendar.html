Claude Co-Work: Automated Weekly Content Calendar

💡 What this does: Uses Claude Co-work's Scheduled Tasks feature to automatically analyze your Instagram account, research viral content, and generate a personalized weekly content calendar — every Monday at 9 AM, hands-free.

🛠️ What You Need

ToolPurposeFree?
Claude Desktop AppRun Co-workYes
Claude for Chrome ExtensionBrowse TikTok & InstagramYes
Claude Co-work (Scheduled Tasks)Automate weekly runsIncluded

⚡ Setup Steps

1️⃣ Install the Tools

  • Download and open the Claude Desktop App
  • Install the Claude for Chrome Extension from the Chrome Web Store
  • The extension is what allows Claude to browse and scroll social platforms in the background

2️⃣ Open Claude Co-work → Scheduled Tasks

  • Switch to Co-work mode inside the Claude desktop app
  • Navigate to the Scheduled section
  • Click New Task

3️⃣ Configure the Task

SettingValue
Task Namedaily-briefing (or your choice)
FrequencyWeekly
DayMonday
Time9:00 AM

4️⃣ Paste the Prompt

Copy the prompt below and paste it into the task description field.

🧠 The Co-Work Prompt

🔍 View Full Prompt — Copy & Paste This
You are my personal content strategist. Every Monday, I want you to complete the following 4-step research and planning workflow and save the output as a document on my computer.

STEP 1 — VIRAL CONTENT RESEARCH:
Browse TikTok and Instagram. Find 10–15 videos in my niche that are performing way above average. For each one, note: the hook (first 3 seconds), the format, the approximate view count, and why you think it blew up.

STEP 2 — PATTERN ANALYSIS:
What are the 3–5 patterns across the top-performing content? What hooks, formats, topics, and emotional triggers are working right now?

STEP 3 — CROSS-REFERENCE WITH MY ACCOUNT AUDIT:
Look at the patterns you found against the gaps in my content. Where are the overlaps? What trending formats could I use to fix my specific weak spots?
For example: if my audit says I need more educational content and the trends show ‘myth-busting’ videos are blowing up — that’s a match. Find those matches.

STEP 4 — MY PERSONALIZED CONTENT CALENDAR:
Build a 7-day content calendar (one post per day). Each day should include:
- Content type (Reel, carousel, talking head, etc.)
- A working title
- The hook (opening line)
- A short outline (3–5 bullet points)
- A caption draft
- Recommended posting time

Save the final document to my Desktop as “Content Calendar — [Week of DATE].pdf”

📊 What Claude Outputs Each Week

OutputDetails
Viral video research10–15 examples with hooks, formats, view counts
Pattern analysisTop 3–5 trends in your niche
Account gap auditWhere your content is falling short
Personalized content calendar7 days, one post per day
Saved filePDF on your Desktop labeled by week

✅ Tips

  • Make sure the Chrome extension is active before the scheduled task runs, or Claude won’t be able to browse social platforms
  • You can add your Instagram handle to the prompt so Claude can audit your specific account
  • Adjust the niche keywords inside the prompt to match your content area (fitness, finance, lifestyle, etc.)
  • Run it manually the first time to verify the output looks right before relying on the weekly schedule
