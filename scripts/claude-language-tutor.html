🌍 Claude Language Tutor — Project Setup

💡 What This Is: A Claude Project that replaces Duolingo, flashcard apps, and expensive tutors — with a 5-minute setup and fully personalized, conversational language learning.

🎯 The Core Concept

Duolingo teaches vocabulary well, but fluency requires conversation, correction, and context. This Claude project fills that gap by acting as an adaptive, always-available language tutor that knows your level and your goals.

⚙️ Setup (5 Minutes)

Step What to Do
1 Create a new Claude Project called Language Tutor
2 Paste the system prompt below into Project Instructions
3 Tell Claude your target language, current level, and conversation goals
4 Start practicing

📝 System Prompt Template

You are my personal language tutor for [LANGUAGE].

My current level: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced]

Conversation goals I want to practice:
- [e.g., ordering food at a restaurant]
- [e.g., small talk with locals]
- [e.g., business meetings]

How to teach me:
- Correct my mistakes word by word, but gently
- Adapt to my level as I improve
- Use vocabulary I've already learned before introducing new words
- When I ask to roleplay, stay in character unless I say "pause"
- After each roleplay session, give me a short debrief: what I did well, what to work on

Start every session by asking what I want to practice today.

🚀 What You Can Do That Duolingo Can't

🏆 Roleplay Real Conversations

Tell Claude to roleplay as a specific character — a barista, a hotel receptionist, a local friend — and practice real back-and-forth dialogue. Claude stays in character and gives live corrections as you go.

<p> <strong>Example prompt:</strong> <em>"Roleplay as a barista in Paris. I'll try to order coffee in French. Correct me if I make mistakes."</em></p></div></details>

✏️ Photo Writing Feedback

Handwrite a sentence or paragraph in your target language, take a photo, and send it to Claude. Claude will break down every mistake word by word — grammar, spelling, word choice.

<p> <strong>Example prompt:</strong> <em>"Here's my handwritten Spanish paragraph. Tell me everything I got wrong."</em></p></div></details>

📚 Custom Story Mode

Ask Claude to write you a short story using only vocabulary you've already learned. Great for reinforcement without overwhelming you with new words.

<p> <strong>Example prompt:</strong> <em>"Write me a 150-word story in Italian using only beginner vocabulary I've likely already seen."</em></p></div></details>

🔄 Adaptive Leveling

Claude continuously tracks your performance and adjusts difficulty. Tell it to push you harder, slow down, or focus on a weak area — it adapts instantly.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Be specific about your goals — "I'm traveling to Japan in 3 months and need restaurant + transit phrases" gives Claude much better context than just "Japanese."
  • Use the photo feature — handwriting practice is one of the most underused Claude capabilities for language learning.
  • Run a weekly debrief — ask Claude to summarize your progress, common mistakes, and suggested focus areas for next week.
  • Create separate projects per language — if you're learning two languages, keep them in separate Claude Projects so context doesn't bleed.
