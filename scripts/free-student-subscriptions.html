5 Free Subscriptions Every Student Needs to Activate Before Graduation

The short version: One student email unlocks thousands of dollars worth of professional tools — most students never claim them. Here are the five that actually matter.

01 — GitHub Student Pack

The single best deal in this list. One student email verification unlocks 100+ professional tools completely free — cloud hosting, domain names, design tools, CI/CD platforms, security scanners, and more. The combined value is well over $200/month.

Why it matters: Many of the tools in this pack — like GitHub Copilot, Namecheap domains, and DigitalOcean credits — are things working developers pay for every month. Getting them free while you're a student means you can build real projects without spending anything.

Apply at education.github.com/pack with your .edu email. Approval usually takes 1–3 days.

02 — Cursor Pro

Cursor is an AI code editor that doesn't just autocomplete — it writes entire features, refactors files, and explains codebases on demand. The Pro plan is normally $192 per year. For students, it's free.

Why it matters: Cursor is what most professional developers are switching to. Learning it as a student means you're already ahead of engineers who haven't made the switch yet. It's the fastest way to go from "I have an idea" to "I have a working app."

Apply at cursor.com/students with your student email.

03 — Notion Pro

Notion's free tier is limited. The Pro plan unlocks AI features, unlimited pages, and full collaboration tools — everything you need to run a second brain, manage projects, and take notes at scale. Free for students.

Why it matters: Notion AI can summarize your notes, generate meeting agendas, draft emails, and answer questions about your own documents. Combined with unlimited storage, it becomes your operating system for university and beyond.

Apply at notion.so/students with a verified student email.

04 — Figma

Figma is the design tool used by product teams at Apple, Google, Airbnb, and virtually every tech company. The full professional plan — including Dev Mode, advanced prototyping, and team libraries — is free for students.

Why it matters: Even if you're not a designer, knowing Figma gives you a massive edge. Developers who can read and work with design files move faster, communicate better with design teams, and can prototype their own ideas without waiting on anyone.

Apply at figma.com/education with your student email.

05 — Perplexity Pro

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine that gives sourced, real-time answers instead of a list of links to click through. The Pro plan adds GPT-4 and Claude access, unlimited file uploads, and deeper research modes. Students get a significant discount.

Why it matters: For research papers, competitive analysis, or just staying informed — Perplexity is faster and more reliable than Google for anything factual. It cites its sources, which matters when you're writing academically.

Check perplexity.ai for current student pricing — discounts are applied at checkout with a student email.

How to claim all five

  1. Use your .edu email wherever possible — it's the master key for most of these
  2. Start with the GitHub Student Pack first — it unlocks tools that overlap with other categories
  3. Verify Notion and Figma with the same student email in one sitting
  4. Apply for Cursor Pro separately at cursor.com/students
  5. Check Perplexity for the latest student discount at checkout
These discounts have expiry dates tied to your graduation year. Set a calendar reminder to re-verify annually — most programs let you renew as long as you're enrolled.
