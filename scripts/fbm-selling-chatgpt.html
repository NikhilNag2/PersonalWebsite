🛍️ FBM Selling w/ ChatGPT

This has to be the craziest ChatGPT use case that I've ever seen.

So you start by taking a picture of something that you want to sell, upload it into ChatGPT and say:

"I need you to sell this for me within the next seven days. It is in used but new condition. I can ship to anyone in the US for $10. Use agent mode."

ChatGPT will then open its own browser, log into your Facebook Marketplace account, and start listing the item for you.

It started building out the listing and it even took the photo I sent it, downloaded it, and then uploaded it as the cover photo. It somehow also found the name of the product and filled in the rest of the details.

It just finished and I'm back on my actual Facebook account. You can see that it filled in everything perfectly — the title, price, condition, description, brand, color, and it even added the $10 shipping.
